ABOUT ME

My story, my journey,
my ambition.

I’m KAZIBWE IVAN, a young and ambitious Ugandan with a strong interest in technology, business, creativity, and continuous learning. Born on 9 March 2003 and raised in Kavumba, Wakiso, my journey has taken me through different experiences in education and work, helping me develop practical skills and a strong desire to grow.

My education began at Bright Future Junior School, followed by St. Mary’s Vocational Secondary School in Kavumba, and later Nakwaaya Secondary School in Mityana, where I completed my secondary education.

I have gained work experience in different fields, including working at SB Stationary in Wakiso, working with Sanitation Systems Uganda on biodigester and septic-tank installations and shallow-well construction, and working as a meat salesman in Nansana Kyebando. These experiences have taught me the value of hard work, adaptability, and learning from every opportunity.

I’m currently interested in Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, and video editing, while continuing to develop myself through technology and new skills. My biggest ambition is to become a highly skilled computer professional who can confidently understand, operate, maintain, and manage computer systems.

Beyond technology, I enjoy music, business, travelling, photography, video editing, and discovering new things. I believe that learning is a continuous journey, and I’m determined to keep improving myself and building a better future.
